# Time Off Policy (Northwind Labs, approved by HR)

Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid time off per calendar year.
In your first calendar year, paid time off is prorated by your start month.
New employees may use paid time off during their first 90 days with their manager's approval.
Paid time off requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance for absences of three days or more.
Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next calendar year; any additional unused days expire on December 31.
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year, separate from paid time off.
The company observes 10 paid public holidays per year, published in the holiday calendar each January.
