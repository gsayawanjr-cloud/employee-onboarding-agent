# Remote Work Policy (Northwind Labs, approved by HR)

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week after completing their first 30 days.
During the first 30 days, new employees work from the office so they can meet their team.
Remote days must be agreed with your manager and recorded in the team calendar.
Working remotely from another country requires written approval from HR at least 30 days in advance.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your office's time zone.
