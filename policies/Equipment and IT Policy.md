# Equipment and IT Policy (Northwind Labs, approved by IT)

Every employee receives a company laptop, which is ready on their first day.
Company laptops must not be used by anyone other than the employee they are assigned to.
Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
Software may only be installed from the company software catalog; other software needs IT approval.
Access to company systems is requested through onboarding and approved by your manager and the system owner.
Equipment must be returned to IT on or before your last day of employment.
