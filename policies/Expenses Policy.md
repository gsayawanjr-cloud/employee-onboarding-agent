# Expenses Policy (Northwind Labs, approved by Finance)

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of purchase, with a receipt.
Expenses over $500 need written approval from your manager before purchase.
Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to $60 per day.
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.
New employees may claim up to $300 for home office equipment in their first 60 days.
Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll after approval.
